Fair Minesweeper Rules

1. The Pure Logic Contract:
You are never required to guess blindly. If you solve everything deductively, you will win every single game.

2. Forced Guess Protection:
Whenever the board reaches an ambiguous 50/50 state where zero tiles can be logically proven safe, whatever tile you choose to guess is 100% guaranteed safe.

3. Unforced Guess Retribution:
If you take a guess while there was at least one tile on the board that was logically deducible as safe, the game actively shifts the hidden mines to guarantee you step on one!

4. Time Machine / Undo:
Made a mistake or got punished? Press Ctrl+Z or click Rewind Death to revert to the exact moment before the fatal click and try another logical move!

5. Controls & Chording:
Desktop: Left-click to reveal, Right-click to flag. Click both buttons, middle-click, or double-click a revealed number to chord (open remaining unflagged neighbors).
Mobile: Tap to reveal. Long-press a hidden tile to flag. Long-press a revealed number to chord!
